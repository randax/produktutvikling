# Heima

Heima is a private home cloud and AI assistant for Norwegian families. This glossary fixes the words we use about its customers and its sales funnel.

## Product

**Heima**:
The overall concept and brand: a family's private cloud and assistant that lives at home. Not something you buy on its own.
_Avoid_: Using "Heima" alone to mean the box or the app

**Heima-boksen**:
The physical device in the Household's home that stores the family's photos and documents and runs the assistant. Sold to the Buyer.
_Avoid_: Server, NAS, router, hub

**Heima-appen**:
The phone app Members use to back up, find and ask about their photos and documents. Sold to the Buyer.
_Avoid_: Client, Heima-app (without the definite ending)

**Private space**:
A Member's own photos and documents, visible only to that Member unless they share something. Everything a Member adds lands here by default.
_Avoid_: Account, personal folder, my files

**Family album**:
The shared collection of photos that every Member of the Household can see and add to.
_Avoid_: Shared library, shared folder, common album
_On the site_: Familiealbumet

**Subscription**:
The small monthly payment a Household makes on top of buying Heima-boksen. It covers the backup copy in Norway, updates and the assistant.
_Avoid_: Plan, membership, licence
_On the site_: abonnementet

**Package**:
One storage size of Heima-boksen together with its matching Subscription price. There are two: Heima 2 TB and Heima 4 TB.
_Avoid_: Plan, tier, bundle, edition
_On the site_: pakke ("Heima 2 TB", "Heima 4 TB")

## Promise

**Digital sovereignty**:
The Household's full ownership of its data: it lives in their home, only they hold the key, no foreign law can reach it, and it stays theirs whatever happens to Heima. Heima's main promise.
_Avoid_: Data sovereignty, privacy (alone), "Norwegian data", security

**Someone else's cloud**:
Any service that keeps the Household's data on the provider's servers where the provider could technically read it, such as iCloud, Google Photos and Jottacloud.
_Avoid_: The cloud, big tech (when Jottacloud is included)

**Household key**:
The secret that encrypts the Household's backup copy. Only the Household's Members can unlock it; Heima never holds it.
_Avoid_: Password, master key, encryption key
_On the site_: familienøkkelen

**Passkey**:
A Member's personal way to unlock the Household key on their own phone, using Face ID or a fingerprint. Every adult Member has one.
_Avoid_: Login, password, PIN
_On the site_: nøkkelen på mobilen

**Key card**:
An optional paper copy of the Household key, kept at home as a last resort if every Passkey is lost.
_Avoid_: Recovery code, backup key
_On the site_: nøkkelkortet

**Continuity guarantee**:
Heima's public, contractual promise that Heima-boksen keeps working locally and all data can be exported, even if the Subscription ends or Heima shuts down.
_Avoid_: End-of-life policy, warranty
_On the site_: Heima-lovnaden («Boksen verkar alltid, og alt er alltid ditt.»)

## People

**Household**:
The unit that subscribes to Heima and shares one Heima installation.
_Avoid_: Family, account, customer (when you mean the subscribing unit)

**Buyer**:
The person in a Household who decides on, reserves and pays for Heima. The primary audience for all marketing.
_Avoid_: Customer, user, lead

**Member**:
Anyone in a Household with their own access to Heima, including the Buyer, grandparents and children.
_Avoid_: User, "family member" (in English internal docs)
_On the site_: familiemedlem

## Funnel

**Reservation**:
A non-binding signal from a Buyer that they want Heima when it launches. Carries no payment or obligation.
_Avoid_: Pre-order, order, waitlist signup
_On the site_: reservasjon, always with «heilt uforpliktande»
